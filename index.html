monolithic systems: immediate functionality, off the print bed

I’m a postdoctoral researcher at the Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT) in Genova. I build monolithic soft robotic systems using parametric design tools — integrating actuation, structure, and sensing into single printed bodies.
